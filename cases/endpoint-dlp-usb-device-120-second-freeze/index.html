Case 06Endpoint DLPEndpoint DLP USB device freeze and the 120-second timeout

USB devices frozen for exactly 120 seconds

Encrypted drives and conferencing devices hung on insert — and every obvious fix targeted the wrong layer.

120 shold deadline
0x102STATUS_TIMEOUT
69hold events logged

Symptom

Two separate cases: a USB conferencing device and a hardware-encrypted USB drive whose launcher apps took minutes to start — or never started — with the endpoint agent enabled.

Investigation

Kernel logs showed the launcher volumes marked controlled and frozen, with every file-open held pending a policy verdict that never arrived — each hold expiring at STATUS_TIMEOUT (0x102) after the 120-second deadline. Explained why the admin's device-control allow rules and app-exclusion lists couldn't help: those act on a different enforcement layer than the content-control hold.

Root cause

No content control policy existed at all. With nothing to evaluate held opens against, the policy engine never produced a verdict, so every open on a frozen volume defaulted to the full timeout.

Fix

Create a content control policy covering removable-device activity. The engine then returned prompt safe-to-open verdicts and holds released instantly — reframing a suspected product defect as a configuration gap, with the layer-by-layer reasoning documented for future cases.
