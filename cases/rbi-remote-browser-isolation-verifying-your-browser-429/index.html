The isolated site stuck on "verifying your browser"

I had a confident first hypothesis. The captures proved me wrong.

429rate-limit status
3 minfrom cache hit to challenge
1hypothesis corrected

Symptom

Users opening a market-news site through Remote Browser Isolation in read-only mode were stuck on a "verifying your browser" interstitial instead of the content — plus a secondary ask to disable the site's chat feature for those users.

Investigation

My first read was a CDN JavaScript challenge conflicting with read-only input suppression. Parsing the working and failing HAR captures corrected it: the challenge came from the site's hosting platform edge, identified by its mitigation response header, a 429 status, and WASM proof-of-work assets. Both captures showed the challenge firing without isolation in the request chain at all.

Root cause

The hosting platform's edge rate-limits by source IP, and shared proxy egress tripped it — three minutes after a clean cached session from the same machine. Not an isolation misconfiguration.

Fix

Site-side egress allowlisting as the durable answer, with isolation-template alternatives assessed as fallbacks. For the chat requirement I identified the exact API endpoint to block — and flagged the WebSocket that must stay open, because it carries the live market feed alongside the chat data.

Real investigation from enterprise Netskope support. Customers and vendors are anonymized.
